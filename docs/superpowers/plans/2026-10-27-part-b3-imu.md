**Goal:** prove IMU behaviour, including faults, closed-loop in `racerbot_sim`, and add the IMU features not built in Part A:
- fused speed for standalone `gap_follow`;
- the understeer check;
- traction control;

Also make adaptive grip earn its place.

**Starting point:** branch `slam-gapfollow-part-a`, merged or branched from.

## Global Constraints

- **The deadman is untouched,** and every new feature gets the four deny-path tests with its flag on.
- **IMU failure falls back to today's behaviour.** Any IMU feature with an IMU that isn't trusted (status `waiting`/`fault`, stale, never received, non-finite) behaves exactly as with the feature off. It names the reason on `/drive_intent`, reusing `gap_follow_node._imu_gate()` from Part A.
- **Flags:** one per feature, plus `imu_all`. With the aggressive policy (memory `aggressive-race-tuning`), a feature ships **on** only if the simulator protocol shows it faster with zero contacts and no added stalls; otherwise it ships off and is noted for a floor trial.
- **Simulator protocol:** as in B-1, on the indoor tracks and `car1_course_2026_10_26`, both directions, at least 2 seeds.
- **The gym's tyres can't slide.** Their force is linear in slip angle (`.sim/f1tenth_gym/.../single_track.py`). Grip and understeer thresholds therefore come from recorded runs, and the simulator checks wiring and fall-back only.
- **`racerbot_sim`'s interlock** must cover the IMU nodes too.

## Review Focus

1. **The IMU drops out mid-corner** with a feature on: the command must equal the feature-off command on that tick, with no jump.
2. **The simulated IMU beside a real IMU stack:** refuse.
3. **The impact stop** must latch until LB is released and pressed again, never clear by itself.
4. **Traction control at standstill or reversing** must not hold acceleration forever.
5. **`imu_all` with the IMU stack absent:** refuse at launch with a clear message.

### Task I1: A simulated IMU in `racerbot_sim`

**Files:** `src/racerbot_sim/racerbot_sim/{sim_bridge.py,gym_bridge_node.py,hardware_guard.py}`, `src/imu_bridge/launch/vehicle_state_launch.py` (`hardware:=false` skips the BNO bridge and `imu_bridge_node`), `src/racerbot_launch/scripts/map_course` (stop forcing `imu:=false` in the simulator).

**What it publishes:** `/imu/bno/data`, `/imu/vesc/data`, `/imu/data` and latched `/imu/status`, in `imu_bridge`'s format, all from the gym's true state:
- **Gyro z:** `state[5]`.
- **Accelerometer:** forward from the change in speed, sideways from speed × yaw rate, and gravity on z.
- **Noise:** measured from a stationary stretch of a real bag (cite the run).

**Faults:** parameter `imu_fault` is one of `none`, `stale`, `status_fault`, `bias`, `scale` or `zero`, and can be set mid-run.

**Tests:**
- On a constant circle, the gyro equals v/R and the sideways acceleration equals v²/R.
- On a ramp, the forward acceleration matches.
- The noise spread matches its setting.
- Each fault gives exactly its output.
- `/imu/status` parses in `imu_health` and `neural_racer`.
- The interlock refuses beside `imu_bridge_node` (Review Focus 2).
- Reproduction: with `odom_yaw_rate_scale` about 1.1, `map_course` without `gyro_odom` fails to close laps and with it succeeds.

### Task I2: Adaptive grip, made to earn its place

**Background (Part A Task 15 results):**
- Even with lag compensation, the simulator's grip ratio sits around 0.91. The gym's linear tyres understeer.
- With relaxed thresholds (hold 0.90, cut 0.85, ceiling 3.5), adaptive grip was slower on car 1's course than plain 2.5 (12.30 s against 12.04 s).
- A fixed 3.0 limit at a 5.0 m/s top speed cost a wall contact.

**Steps:**
- Replay car 1's recorded runs (`20261026-194514`, `-194913`) through `gap_logic.adaptive_grip_step` offline, to see what limit it would have chosen on the real floor. The ROS-free functions take the recorded yaw rate, speed and commanded steering.
- Use I1's simulated IMU to check the fall-backs closed-loop.
- Decide ship-on or ship-off by the aggressive policy, and record why.

### Task I3: Fused speed in `gap_follow_launch.py`

`fused_speed:=true` points `gap_follow`'s `odom_topic` at `/odom/fused` and starts the IMU stack, mirroring `map_course`'s existing flag (`odom_overrides`). Tests follow `test_map_course_imu.py`'s pattern.

### Task I4: The understeer check (`imu_understeer`)

Slow down when `/steering/measured` shows the car turning much less than commanded. The input is NaN below 0.3 m/s, which means no action. Thresholds come from the recorded runs' grip bands (`analysis/report.md` in `20261026-194514`).

### Task I5: Traction control (`imu_traction`)

Hold the acceleration ramp while the wheel speed (`/odom`) outruns the fused speed (`/odom/fused`) by more than a threshold. Release after a timeout so the ramp is never held forever (Review Focus 4).

### Task I6: The impact stop (`imu_impact_stop`)

An accelerometer spike above a threshold, taken from recorded bumps (find one in the bags, or record one wheels-off), commands zero speed and latches until LB is released and pressed again (Review Focus 3). This is a safety addition, so its deny paths are the main tests.

### Task I7: `imu_all` in `gap_follow_launch.py`

`imu_all:=true` turns on every IMU flag in `gap_follow_launch.py`, and an explicit `false` overrides it. It's the same shortcut `map_course` gets in B-2's Task S7.

**Each feature task I3-I6:**
- ROS-free logic in `gap_logic.py` with closed-form tests.
- Node wiring through `_imu_gate`.
- The four IMU failure modes give exactly the feature-off command (Review Focus 1).
- The four deadman deny paths with the flag on.
- Mirror parity with the flag off, plus a flag-on mirror test.
- The simulator protocol, and docs.
